\# Network Sniffing Lab Notes



These notes summarize my learning from EC-Council CEH v13 Module 08: Sniffing. I completed the exercises in an authorized course lab.



\## What I learned



\- Use Wireshark concepts to inspect network traffic and follow TCP streams.

\- Understand how switches learn MAC addresses and use CAM tables.

\- Learn about MAC flooding, DHCP starvation, and ARP poisoning in a lab setting.

\- Study DNS spoofing, proxy poisoning, and VLAN hopping.

\- Review defenses such as switch port security, DNSSEC, encrypted communication, IDS monitoring, and secure network configuration.



\## Hands-on practice



I completed lab exercises involving:



\- MAC flooding with `macof`

\- DHCP starvation with Yersinia

\- Wireshark traffic and TCP stream analysis

\- ARP poisoning and promiscuous-mode detection



\## Tools and concepts



Wireshark, Nmap NSE, `macof`, Yersinia, TCP streams, CAM tables, ARP, DHCP, DNS, and VLANs.
