\# Wazuh Security Monitoring Lab



\## Objective



Use Wazuh in a lab or training environment to practice reviewing security events and monitoring endpoint activity.



\## Environment



\- Personal lab or training environment

\- Test devices and authorized systems only



\## Tool Used



\- Wazuh



\## What I Practiced



\- Navigating the Wazuh dashboard

\- Reviewing security alerts

\- Examining endpoint events

\- Investigating file-integrity monitoring activity

\- Checking alert details and severity

\- Recording findings and recommended next steps



\## What I Learned



Wazuh supports security monitoring by collecting and analyzing endpoint security events. Reviewing alerts involves checking the event details, understanding the affected system, and deciding whether further investigation or remediation is needed.



\## Ethical Scope



This work was completed in a personal lab or authorized training environment. It represents hands-on lab experience, not a claim of production SOC administration.
