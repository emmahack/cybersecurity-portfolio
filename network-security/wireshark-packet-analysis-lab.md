\# Wireshark Packet Analysis Lab



\## Objective



Use Wireshark to capture and examine network traffic in an authorized lab or training environment.



\## Environment



\- Personal lab computer

\- Training platform or course lab

\- Authorized systems only



\## Tool Used



\- Wireshark



\## What I Practiced



\- Capturing network traffic

\- Filtering packets to focus an investigation

\- Reviewing DNS, HTTP, and other network activity

\- Identifying source and destination information

\- Looking for unusual or suspicious traffic patterns



\## What I Learned



Wireshark helps security analysts investigate network activity by showing how systems communicate. Packet analysis can support troubleshooting, incident investigation, and identification of suspicious traffic.



\## Ethical Scope



All traffic capture and analysis was performed only in personal, training, or otherwise authorized environments.
