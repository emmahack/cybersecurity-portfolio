\# Nmap Service Discovery Lab



\## Objective



Use Nmap to identify open ports and services on systems I owned, training platforms, or other systems I had permission to scan.



\## Environment



\- Personal lab computer

\- Training platform or course lab

\- Authorized systems only



\## Tool Used



\- Nmap



\## What I Practiced



\- Identifying reachable hosts

\- Checking open ports

\- Identifying services running on open ports

\- Reviewing scan results for possible security risks



\## What I Learned



Nmap helps security professionals understand which services are exposed on a system. This information can be used to reduce unnecessary exposure, review firewall rules, and investigate potential security risks.



\## Ethical Scope



All scanning was performed only on systems I owned, training environments, or systems where I had permission to scan.
